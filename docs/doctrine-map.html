Doctrine → enforcement

One row per doctrinal claim in this repo: where it is stated, and what makes it true. The unenforced rows are the deliverable. A claim with no enforcement is not automatically a defect — plenty are judgement, and gating judgement is worse than not gating it. The value is making the distinction visible instead of discovering it by accident.
