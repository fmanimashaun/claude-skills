- **A CONFIRMED verdict quotes only what the cited page says: `scripts/check_verdict_quotes.py` matches every quote against the page's raw text** (#2233). `doctrine-verifier` read pages through a summarising fetch, so a CONFIRMED verdict could rest on a paraphrase or on text from a page it never saw (#2220 measured six of 49 quoted phrases absent from their pages, and one claim confirmed from a JavaScript shell). The verdict now carries a fenced `quotes` block (`url | version or date | phrase`); the script fetches each page, drops `<script>`/`<style>`/comments, strips tags, normalises whitespace, curly quotes and dashes, and refuses an absent phrase (exit 1); a page that serves no text of its own, or cannot be fetched, is INCONCLUSIVE (exit 2), never CONFIRMED. `.claude/agents/doctrine-verifier.md` requires the block and the run. `maintainer_doctor.py` runs the offline selftest (a real page, a fabricated quote, a paraphrase, a wrong-case quote, script-only text, a shell, a missing page) and `--format-check`, which refuses a new audit (dated 2026-10-22 or later) that says CONFIRMED with no `quotes` block and no written `quotes-waived` reason. Re-run over the audits already in `docs/evidence/audits/` with `--legacy`: 35 quoted phrases in 7 CONFIRMED audits, 26 matched by the script, 9 not; by hand, 1 was a real mismatch and 1 a capitalisation slip (both corrected in `docs/evidence/audits/2026-10-10-pwa-doctrine-1750.md`), 5 were present in sources the check had no URL for, and 2 were not source quotes. The checker is a verifier's tool and needs a network, so it is not a CI gate; the gates are its selftest and the format check.
