- **rails-stack declares its security surface for `/rails-flow:secure-design` — `plugins/rails-stack/security/rails.md`, `plugins/rails-stack/stacks.json`** (#2117). Its descriptor's new `security` block names the Rails STRIDE checklist (questions pointing at existing rails-8 doctrine; no new claims), the scanners the stack runs (brakeman, bundler-audit, importmap audit), and the Rails spellings and paths that mean a boundary (`allow_unauthenticated_access`, `html_safe`, `redirect_to params`, `app/controllers/...`), moved out of the neutral `security_scope.py`; `security/boundary-cases.json` proves them. The auth paths match any sessions, passwords, registrations, logins, magic-link, two-factor, invitation, confirmation or unlock controller in any namespace, plus `application_controller.rb`, `concerns/auth*`, `models/user.rb`, the devise and session-store initializers and `lib/**/auth*`; upload, attachment, import and MCP controllers count; `render html:`/`inline:` and `redirect_to request.referer` are boundaries; each has a case.
