section: Repository hygiene
- **An advice row in the doctrine map must stand on something — `scripts/doctrine_map.py`** (#2224, owner ruling 2026-10-21: "advice is not bad but must be tested against best practices or standards").
  - **The new field.** A `Claim` gains a `basis`: either `source: <what was checked> @ <version in scope>`, or `decision: #<n>` (a maintainer decision, which `refs` must cite).
  - **What is refused.** An advice row with neither is `unbased advice`. A basis of the wrong form is `malformed basis`. A decision `refs` does not cite is `decision not cited`.
  - **The baseline.** Measured when the ruling landed: 16 advice rows, of which 4 had a basis (two style.md rows verified on rubocop 1.91.0 (#1363); `git add -A` decision #1793; the MCP checklist decision #2056) and 12 had neither. The 12 are `ADVICE_UNBASED`, a list that may only shrink: an entry whose row gains a basis, becomes a guarantee or is deleted is a finding until it is removed.
  - **"May only shrink" is checked against the base, not against itself** (#2254 review). `--check` reads the base's `scripts/doctrine_map.py` (`origin/dev`, or `DOCTRINE_MAP_BASE`) with `git show`, and refuses any entry the base did not allow: its `ADVICE_UNBASED`, or, before the list existed, its advice rows. So a new unbased row cannot ship with its own grandfather entry. The comparison point is `git merge-base <base> HEAD`, and HEAD's first parent when that is HEAD itself (a push to `dev`, or `DOCTRINE_MAP_BASE=HEAD` by hand), so a list never agrees with itself. An unreadable base is a finding, never a pass. A `source:` basis must also cite the verdict or issue that checked it in `refs`.
  - **Proof.** The selftest is 62 cases, including a new row plus its new entry against a base fixture, both base shapes, an unreadable base, and the real list against the real rows. The guard is 18 mutations, all caught.
