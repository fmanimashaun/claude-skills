- **`rails-8` doctrine moves its PostgreSQL baseline from 16 and 17 to 18 — `skills/rails-8/references/deployment-kamal.md`, `skills/rails-8/references/observability.md`, `skills/rails-8/references/models.md`** (#2057). The Kamal accessory example now uses `postgres:18` and tells the reader the data path depends on the image's major: from 18 the official image declares `VOLUME /var/lib/postgresql` and `PGDATA=/var/lib/postgresql/18/docker` (17 and earlier keep `/var/lib/postgresql/data`), a mount at the old path on 18 is not `PGDATA`, and a major version is never a tag bump (dump and restore or `pg_upgrade`). The version notes in `observability.md` and `models.md` are re-based on the PostgreSQL 18 docs, `stats_since`/`minmax_stats_since` are stated as present since 17, 18's constant-list squashing is noted, and one sentence is **corrected**: `queryid` splits when a referenced *function* is dropped and recreated, not a table (a recreated table can make two queries count as the same). Verified by `doctrine-verifier` against the PostgreSQL 16.0, 17.0, 18.0 release notes and 18 docs and the Docker Hub `postgres` page (`docs/evidence/audits/2026-10-16-postgres-18-baseline-2057.md`); the data-loss consequence of a `/data` mount on 18 and three other sentences are INCONCLUSIVE and left as they were or labelled; the design decision is the owner's (everything on 18, 2026-10-14).
