- **System specs: Playwright is documented as a declared alternative driver, and Selenium stays mandated — `skills/rails-8/references/testing.md`** (#1441). doctrine-verifier (2026-10-12): Rails ≥ 7.2 ships `driven_by :playwright` (rails/rails `actionpack/lib/action_dispatch/system_testing/driver.rb`, 8-1-stable lists `:selenium, :cuprite, :rack_test, :playwright`); it needs `capybara-playwright-driver` (0.5.12, 2026-09-28), the Node `playwright-core` package and an installed browser; the driver's README, Rails integration guide (https://playwright-ruby-client.vercel.app/docs/article/guides/rails_integration) and open issues list its differences from Selenium; and Lexxy's move (https://dev.37signals.com/lexxy-1-0/) was off Capybara, so it is not evidence for a driver swap. Measured on Retask (a 20-file sample of 123 browser spec files, 3 runs per driver, 2026-10-12): the 53 portable examples passed on both drivers every run, about a minute each warm, no flakes; 83 of the 123 files call `page.driver.browser` (Selenium API, CDP) and fail under Playwright (`NoMethodError: private method 'browser'`). Decision (coordinator claude-skills-4d, a tooling choice, recorded on #1441): keep the Selenium mandate; Playwright is a project's declared choice, recorded in its CLAUDE.md "Project Overrides", after counting the specs that reach into `page.driver.browser`. `dist/rails-8.skill` rebuilt.
