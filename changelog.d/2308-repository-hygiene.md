section: Repository hygiene
- **A cancelled or failed gate sweep and mutation guard now always name what they were running or what failed — `scripts/maintainer_doctor.py`, `scripts/mutation_check.py`** (#2308). PR #2301's `gates` job was cancelled at the 25-minute PR timeout and left no output, because the sweep printed its results only in `report()` at the very end, so a slow runner and a hung gate looked the same. The sweep now writes `gate start: <name> (limit N s)` and `gate end: <name> <passed|timed out|exit N> in X s` as each gate runs, flushed at once (CI pipes stdout, where an unflushed line dies with the process); SIGTERM, which a cancelled job receives after SIGINT, is read as an interrupt for the sweep (`cancelled_as_interrupt`), so the gate's whole process group is killed and `gate interrupted: <name> after X s` is written. A guard's report of a red selftest (`tail_block`) was the last 6 to 12 lines, so a `1 of 187 checks FAILED` tally with ten lines after it, or a list longer than the tail, lost the label of the failing check, and three re-runs passed so it could not be named afterwards; it now always carries the tally and every `  - label` line (up to 40) with the tail, and a starved entry prints whole. `scripts/maintainer_doctor_selftest.py` runs the real sweep over a fast and a slow gate and cancels it mid-gate with SIGTERM and with SIGKILL, and `scripts/mutation_check_selftest.py` drives the label cases; four mutants in `scripts/mutations/maintainer_doctor.py` and three in `scripts/mutations/mutation_check_harness.py` each turn their own check red. Part 2 of the issue (chase the slow gate once it can be named) waits for a cancelled run to name one.
