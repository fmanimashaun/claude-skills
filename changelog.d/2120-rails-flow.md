section: rails-flow
- **A `production-config` gate reads `config/environments/production.rb` — `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/check_production_config.py`** (#2120, part of the secure-by-design audit #2115). Production configuration was validated by nothing in the toolchain, although `auth-security.md` documents the worst trap (`config.hosts` is empty in production and does nothing); a downstream app shipped with it commented out (Retask #2060: Host-header and DNS-rebinding protection off, an emailed link built from the request host). The check is static and offline and reads only uncommented code in `production.rb`, `application.rb`, `config/initializers/*.rb` and `config/routes.rb`: it fails when `config.hosts` is unset (`= nil`, `= []` and `.clear` count as unset), when `config.force_ssl = true` is absent (`assume_ssl` alone is not it), when `consider_all_requests_local` is true, on a wildcard `origins` in a Rack::Cors block (matched structurally, not by a bare `*`), and on a dev-only engine or route (letter_opener, rails_db, GraphiQL, `/rails/info`, `/rails/mailers`) mounted outside a development guard. Exemptions are declared per rule, with a non-empty reason, in `.rails-flow/production-config.json` (an unknown rule, a missing reason and an exemption for a rule that passes are findings; the `hosts` reason can say the host is filtered at the proxy, named). Credential literals are not checked: secret detection is #2121. `plugins/rails-flow/checks.json` gains the `production-config` entry (applies when `config/environments/production.rb` exists), with a `--selftest` and a mutation guard. Maintainer decision: scope and the one-exemption-file shape confirmed on #2120 by the coordinator (credential-literal rule dropped in favour of #2121).
