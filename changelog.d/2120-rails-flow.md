section: rails-flow
- **A `production-config` gate reads `config/environments/production.rb` — `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/check_production_config.py`** (#2120, part of the secure-by-design audit #2115). Production configuration was validated by nothing in the toolchain, although `auth-security.md` documents the worst trap (`config.hosts` is empty in production and does nothing); a downstream app shipped with it commented out (Retask #2060: Host-header and DNS-rebinding protection off, an emailed link built from the request host). The check is static and offline. It evaluates `config/application.rb`, then `config/environments/production.rb`, then `config/initializers/*.rb` as ONE sequence in Rails' load order (the last setting wins, so production.rb's `config.hosts = nil` or `.clear` empties what application.rb set), after a Ruby-aware scanner removes comments only (strings, `#{}`, `%w[]`, regexps, heredocs and `=begin`/`=end` are understood). A setting made only conditionally (an `if`/`unless`/`case` block, a trailing modifier, a ternary, an `ENV` value) does not count as made. Rules: `hosts` non-empty (`=`, `<<`, `+=`, `.push`, `.concat` count; `= nil`, `= []`, `.clear` empty); `config.force_ssl = true` literally and unconditionally (`assume_ssl` alone is not it); `consider_all_requests_local` not true (a conditional or non-literal value is unjudgeable and a finding); no wildcard Rack::Cors origin (`"*"`, `%w[*]`, a constant or variable bound to one, `/.*/` or `/.+/`, a block returning `true`; matched structurally, not by a bare `*`); no dev-only engine or route (letter_opener, rails_db, GraphiQL, `/rails/info`, `/rails/mailers`) outside a development guard, and no admin or job dashboard (Sidekiq::Web, MissionControl::Jobs::Engine, PgHero, Blazer, Flipper::UI) outside a development guard or an access constraint (`constraints`/`authenticate`). Exemptions are declared per rule, with a non-empty reason, in `.rails-flow/production-config.json` (an unknown rule, a missing reason and an exemption for a rule that passes are findings; the `hosts` reason can say the host is filtered at the proxy, named). Credential literals are not checked: secret detection is #2121. `plugins/rails-flow/checks.json` gains the `production-config` entry (applies when `config/environments/production.rb` exists), with a `--selftest` and a mutation guard. Maintainer decision: scope and the one-exemption-file shape confirmed on #2120 by the coordinator (credential-literal rule dropped in favour of #2121); the load-order, conditional, scanner and CORS/dashboard widening are the independent review of #2134.
