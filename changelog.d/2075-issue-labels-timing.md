- **The `issue_labels` linear-time selftest no longer fails a release on one noisy timing — `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/scripts/lib/issue_labels.py`** (#2075). The selftest proves linear time by timing a half-size and a full-size input and requiring a ratio under 3.0. On a shared runner one measurement came out at 3.4 (v1.157.2's release run), which turned the guard `hook_issue_labels` INERT and failed the release's mutation job. A borderline ratio is now measured again, up to three attempts keeping the lowest; a quadratic verdict measures about 4 or more every time, so it is still refused (the guard's three quadratic mutants stay caught). Maintainer tooling only; no behaviour of the shipped hook changes.
