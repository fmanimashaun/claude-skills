section: qa-flow
- **The robot helper-test runner's decisions are pure functions with rows that can fail, and a mutation guard proves it — `plugins/qa-flow/scripts/scaffold_ts_tests.py`** (#1979). The runner decides pass or fail for the scaffolded TypeScript helpers and six guards lean on it as their selftest, but its own rules (a run with no tests is not a pass, a failing test is not a pass, the Node floor, the CI exit-1 rule) had no selftest rows beyond the CI one and no guard of their own. `run_passed`, `too_old` and `no_tests` are now pure functions with 16 selftest rows, `--selftest-only` runs them without Node, and `plugins/qa-flow/scripts/mutations/scaffold_ts_tests.py` has ten mutants (zero tests as a pass, failures ignored, a non-zero exit ignored, an invented footer, the floor lowered, the floor itself too old, an unreadable version usable, the CI rule dropped, a blank CI value as CI, an empty list as tests); all ten are caught, in 1 s on macOS and in the Linux container (`scripts/linux_check.sh scaffold_ts_tests`). The six dependent guards are unchanged. Our own design; no framework claim.
