section: rails-flow
- **guard-pr-ready.sh no longer takes quadratic time on a long command, and both it and guard-worktree.sh refuse an oversize one in time, with a reason — `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/scripts/guard-pr-ready.sh`, `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/scripts/guard-worktree.sh`, `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/check_hook_gates.py`, `scripts/mutations/hook_guard_pr_ready_scope.py`, `scripts/mutations/hook_guard_worktree.py`** (#1980). guard-pr-ready.sh walked the command's lines with `${rest#*$'\n'}` slicing, which bash 3.2 does in quadratic time: a command of plain lines took 60 s at 158 KB and 83 s at 211 KB, past the hook's 15 s timeout, which is a DENY since #1968, with no reason given. It now reads the lines with `read` from a here-string, the cure guard-worktree.sh already had from #1978 (158 KB in 1.2 s, 1 MB in 9.8 s, linear). guard-worktree.sh was already linear on dev, so the 16 s the issue measured did not reproduce there; its fix here is the cap and the fixture. Both gates now also refuse a command past 400,000 characters at once, saying it is too large to read and to write the content to a file instead, because even a linear read of a megabyte approaches the timeout. `check_hook_gates.py` gains a 170 KB plain-lines fixture for each gate with an 8 s bound, a 170 KB `gh pr ready` that must be judged, and an oversize case; the mutants restore the slicing walk and remove the cap, in each guard. Maintainer decision: #1980, the issue's own Fix section; no framework claim.
