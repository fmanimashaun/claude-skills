section: Repository hygiene
- **A PR adds `changelog.d/<issue>-<slug>.md` instead of editing `CHANGELOG.md`, and the arm folds the fragments in — `scripts/changelog_fragments.py`, `changelog.d/README.md`, `scripts/extract_release_notes.py`, `scripts/check_arm_window.py`, `scripts/maintainer_doctor.py`** (#1825, the coordinator's assignment). Every PR added its bullet at the top of the same `### Unreleased`, so two PRs open at once conflicted on it: of 85 merges into `dev` since 2026-10-09, 67 (79%) touched `CHANGELOG.md`, and a hand-resolved conflict has dropped a bullet before (#728). A new file cannot conflict with another PR's new file. `--check` (a doctor gate) holds a fragment to the rule the CHANGELOG's own bullets are held to (it names a path of its section's component, read from `lint_self_consistency.py`); `--fold` puts each under its section's `### Unreleased`, deterministic, refusing a dirty CHANGELOG and any fold that cannot place every fragment, and `--fold --into vX.Y.Z` is the existing "fold into the armed block" repair as a command. `extract_release_notes.py --promotion` refuses an unfolded fragment, and `check_arm_window.py` names the repair when one arrives while `dev` is armed. A bullet added directly to `### Unreleased` is still accepted for now. Our own design; the decision is recorded on #1825.
