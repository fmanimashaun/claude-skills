- **The doctor prints each gate's wall time in the sweep output — `scripts/maintainer_doctor.py`** (#2246). The sweep log had no per-gate time: every `[  ok  ]` line carried the end-of-run timestamp, so a gate near its budget (the `neutral proof ratchet` was the case) could not be read from CI. The doctor now times each gate on its own clock, a killed or failing gate included, and prints a block before the totals: each gate of a second or more with its seconds and its share of the sweep, slowest first, the gates under a second summed on one line, and the count and total of the gates that ran. The `[  ok  ]`, `[ FAIL ]` and `[ skip ]` lines are unchanged, so nothing that reads them changes. Selftest rows drive the lines directly and through a real `check_gates` and `report`; seven guard mutations cover them.
