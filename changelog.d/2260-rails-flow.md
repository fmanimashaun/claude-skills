section: rails-flow
- **The security-review reader honours a language's line-comment markers and refuses a file it cannot read safely — `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/check_security_review.py`, `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/detect_stack.py`** (#2260). A `security.code_syntax` row may now declare `line_comments` (markers that make a line starting with them a comment) and `refuse_when` (a pattern which, on any line, refuses the whole file, with a reason), and `heredoc`, `line_comments` and `refuse_when` are optional in a row; the descriptor validator checks that each `refuse_when` pattern compiles and carries a `why`. `non_code_lines` takes the markers, `refusal_for` applies the refusal before a cited line is judged, and the syntax-case runner understands `refused` and `declared` cases. Selftest fixtures cover a row's own marker, a refusal that names its line, and that neither leaks to another file type; the guards gain eight mutations. The copies of `detect_stack.py` in `pipeline` and `design-flow` follow byte for byte. The version is assigned at promotion.
