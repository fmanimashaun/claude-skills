section: rails-flow
- **The adversary verdict is enforced: `risky_diff.py` is registered in `plugins/rails-flow/checks.json` as `adversary-review`, so the sweep that `gh pr ready` requires fails on a risky diff with no current record** (#2264, owner direction: everything must be enforced). The record is found from the branch name (`docs/evidence/reviews/prs/<slug>/adversary.md`) with no flag; no record, a stale one or an unusable one exits 1; a branch with no name or a base that does not resolve (`origin/<base>` is tried too) exits 2. A BLOCKED verdict now exits 1 instead of 3, because the project gates read exit 3 as not applicable and would pass a blocking finding (#1873); a human lets it through with `Override: <who> | <reason, 20+ characters> | #<issue>` in the record, the form `advisory_scope.py` takes for RETHINK (an override that names nobody, a short or filler reason, or an issue `gh` cannot read is refused). `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/advisory_scope.py` shares that line, and its override pattern now also refuses a `who` that is only spaces. `feature.md` and `fix.md` say so. Every refusal arm has a selftest case and a guard mutation in `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/mutations/risky_diff.py`.
