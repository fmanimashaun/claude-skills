- **A Fable plan advisory before the build, required and enforced when the plan crosses a boundary — `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/advisory_scope.py`, `plugins/rails-flow/agents/advisor.md`** (#2262). Our-design change, no external claim: owner direction of 2026-10-21 ("we should make fable advisory a standard flow in our plugin"), with the coordinator's rulings recorded on #2262. A new `advisor` agent (`model: fable`, read-only, at most seven findings with the most consequential first, then `VERDICT: PROCEED` or `VERDICT: RETHINK`) reviews the unit's plan files: criteria, work order, spec, threat model and slice plan. `advisory_scope.py` says when it is required: the plan names a security boundary (it reuses `security_scope.py`), changes structure (a plugin, agent, command, skill, hook, gate or descriptor key), touches the data model (the detected stack's `layout.migrations` and `layout.schema`) or a dependency, or spans six or more components. Breadth was measured 2026-10-21 on the 30 most recent PRs merged into dev, bookkeeping excluded: 0-3 components 11, 4: 4, 5: 6, 6+: 9. The record `docs/evidence/reviews/plans/<slug>/advisor.md` must cover the plan as it is now. Every finding needs exactly one disposition (accepted, rejected with a non-filler reason, or deferred to `#n`). RETHINK blocks until a fresh pass says PROCEED or an override names who decided, a reason of 20+ characters and an issue. `--stamp` accepts a revised plan only when its categories are a subset of the reviewed ones and at most 30% of its lines changed. That threshold was measured on 66 revisions of 53 Retask acceptance files: median 9%, 75th percentile 22%, 90th percentile 52%. Enforced twice: `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/scripts/stop-gate.sh` blocks once criteria exist, and `checks.json` (id `plan-advisory`) puts it in the sweep that `gh pr ready` requires. `/rails-flow:feature`, `/rails-flow:fix`, `/rails-flow:slice` and `/rails-flow:secure-design` call it. `plugins/rails-flow/reference/model-tiers.md` makes `advisor` the second deliberate `fable` pin, beside `adversary`.
