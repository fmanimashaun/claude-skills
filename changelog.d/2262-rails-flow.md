- **A Fable plan advisory before the build, required and enforced when the plan crosses a boundary — `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/advisory_scope.py`, `plugins/rails-flow/agents/plan-advisor.md`** (#2262). Our-design change, no external claim: owner direction of 2026-10-21 ("we should make fable advisory a standard flow in our plugin"), with the coordinator's rulings recorded on #2262. A new `plan-advisor` agent (`model: fable`, read-only, at most seven findings with the most consequential first, then `VERDICT: PROCEED` or `VERDICT: RETHINK`) reviews the unit's plan files: criteria, work order, spec, threat model and slice plan. `advisory_scope.py` says when it is required: the plan names a security boundary (it reuses `security_scope.py`), changes structure (a plugin, agent, command, skill, hook, gate or descriptor key), touches the data model (the detected stack's `layout.migrations` and `layout.schema`) or a dependency, or spans six or more components. Breadth was measured 2026-10-21 on the 30 most recent PRs merged into dev, bookkeeping excluded: 0-3 components 11, 4: 4, 5: 6, 6+: 9. The record `docs/evidence/reviews/plans/<slug>/plan-advisor.md` must cover the plan as it is now. Every finding needs exactly one disposition (accepted, rejected with a non-filler reason, or deferred to `#n`). RETHINK blocks until a fresh pass says PROCEED or an override names who decided, a reason of 20+ characters and an issue. `--stamp` accepts a revised plan only when its categories are a subset of the reviewed ones and at most 30% of its lines changed. That threshold was measured on 66 revisions of 53 Retask acceptance files: median 9%, 75th percentile 22%, 90th percentile 52%. Enforced twice: `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/scripts/stop-gate.sh` blocks once criteria exist, and `checks.json` (id `plan-advisory`) puts it in the sweep that `gh pr ready` requires. `/rails-flow:feature`, `/rails-flow:fix`, `/rails-flow:slice` and `/rails-flow:secure-design` call it. `plugins/rails-flow/reference/model-tiers.md` makes `plan-advisor` the second deliberate `fable` pin, beside `adversary`. **Review:** the agent is `plan-advisor`, not to be confused with Claude Code's built-in advisor. An answer with no `A-n` finding and no `Findings: none` line, or with more than seven findings, is refused. The reviewed-plan snapshot must hash to `Plan-SHA256`, which stops an accidental refresh of either one; it cannot stop a deliberate forgery that rewrites both. An override's issue must be readable with `gh issue view`, failing closed. A branch with no prefix is its own slug. The stop gate fails closed when the plugin root lacks `advisory_scope.py`. **Dogfood plan-advisor pass** (the record is `docs/evidence/reviews/plans/2262-plan-advisory/plan-advisor.md`). A new PreToolUse tripwire, `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/scripts/guard-plan.sh`, refuses a build edit outside `docs/` on a flow branch while the advisory is owed. It fails open: the stop gate and the sweep are the guarantee. `security` now fires only when the unit's threat model has threat rows. Re-measured on Retask's 53 plans, the trigger fires on 16 (30%), down from 31 (58%). `--begin` refuses over an existing record, and `--again` keeps the old one numbered. `applies_when` is `docs`. Deferred: a maintainer-flow plan step (#2269), and enforcing the slice advisory in the gh shim (#2270).
