section: rails-flow
- **The mock-up record names its page template, states, screen, place in the navigation and, once built, its story — `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/check_mockup_gate.py`, `plugins/rails-flow/reference/mockup-vocabulary.json`** (#2226, the owner's "everything must be enforced"; our own design, no framework claim). One list of allowed values (`mockup-vocabulary.json`: templates, system sub-kinds, states, a nav depth of 3) is read by the gate and cited by the frontend architecture framework; a missing list refuses every record. A record that is added or changed is refused without a `Template:`, with an unknown one, with a `States:` line that is not `;`-separated entries covering every state exactly once as `<state>` or `<state>: n/a: <reason>` (an empty reason, a duplicate, or all states n/a is refused), without a unique `Screen:`, or with a `Nav:` that is not `level N under <parent>` (N from 1 to 3, level 1 under `shell`, level N under the `Screen:` of an existing level N-1 record). On the real diff (`--base`) the record must also name a `Story:` that is a story file or a user-visible file in the repo; at plan time (`--paths`) it need not. `check_mockup_gate.py --audit` is the online check: a record with a `Template:` whose issue is closed and that names no `Story:` fails, an unreadable issue is unjudged (exit 2), legacy records are only listed. **Migration:** existing records are never re-judged; a record that is next changed needs the new lines (Retask's free-text `States:` lines will be refused then); a Next.js project without Storybook cannot satisfy `Story:` until the stack descriptor declares its UI paths (#2219). The reader and the audit are one file, `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/mockup_records.py`, kept byte-identical in qa-flow.
