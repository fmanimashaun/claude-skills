section: qa-flow
- **The release-image QA robot keeps persona and records manifests and proves its teardown — `plugins/qa-flow/scaffold/qa/e2e/support/personas.ts`, `plugins/qa-flow/scaffold/qa/e2e/support/teardown.ts`** (#1836, slice 2 of #1693). Lifted from Retask #826 and made generic. `personas.ts`: a persona manifest (handle, role, QA address, browser-state file) and a records manifest (kind, id, the QA persona that made it), in `QA_SEED_DIR` (default `qa/.qa-seed`), written owner-only (`chmod 0600` after every write, since a create-time mode leaves an existing wider file wide); a non-QA address is refused on write and on read in both manifests, a duplicate handle or a record without kind/id is refused. `teardown.ts`: `proveNothingLeft(<compose project>)` reads docker back after the stack's own `down` and fails on any container or volume of that project (exact name, or the `-`/`_` separators, never a bare substring), and **fails closed when docker cannot be asked** (non-zero exit or not on PATH) — the defect Retask's first version had, where `|| true` turned a failed query into "0 left". 14 new `node:test` cases (30 in all), including the issue's two required fixtures (a leftover → fail; docker unavailable → fail), and two mutation guards (10 mutants). `/qa-flow:setup-qa` copies both. Our own design: owner decision recorded on #1693.
