section: Repository hygiene
- **A shipped doc may not carry a Vercel or Supabase CLI step without a hand-off line — `scripts/check_vendor_handoff.py`** (#2289, plugin-boundaries rule 2: one home per concern). A line of a tracked `.md` under `plugins/` or `skills/` that tells the reader to run `supabase <step>` or `vercel <step>` (also behind `npx`) is refused unless that same line is a hand-off line: it carries `Hand-off:` and names the vendor skill, command or plugin it hands off to. The excuse is per line, so a copied table cannot hide beside a pointer. The maintainer doctor runs it and its `--selftest` (a hand-off line alone is clean; every way around it is a fixture); its mutation guard `scripts/mutations/check_vendor_handoff.py` has 12 mutations.
