section: Repository hygiene
- **A shipped doc may not carry a Vercel or Supabase CLI step without a hand-off line — `scripts/check_vendor_handoff.py`** (#2289, plugin-boundaries rule 2: one home per concern). A line of a tracked `.md` under `plugins/` or `skills/` that tells the reader to run a vendor step (`supabase <step>` or `vercel <step>`, also behind `npx`, `vercel --prod`, a bare `vercel` where it is told to be run) is refused, and a line that says `Hand-off:` does not excuse it: a pointer names the vendor skill or command and carries no command of its own, so a table with `Hand-off:` on every row is still a copied table. A `Hand-off:` line about Vercel or Supabase must name its vendor target. The maintainer doctor runs it and its `--selftest` (a hand-off line alone is clean; every way around it is a fixture); its mutation guard `scripts/mutations/check_vendor_handoff.py` has 22 mutations.
