- **A task-list mock-up record can declare the words its page shows for task statuses — `plugins/rails-flow/reference/mockup-record.md`** (#2327). An optional `Statuses:` line on a `Template: multi-step: task-list` record, `Statuses: not-started=To do; cannot-start=Blocked; completed=Done`, with roles from the new `task_statuses` list in `mockup-vocabulary.json`; `cannot-start` and `completed` are required once the line exists, one word names one role, and no other record takes the line. `check_mockup_gate.py` refuses a malformed line when a record is added or changed (`statuses_problems`, with a refusal fixture for each of the nine ways it can be wrong), and refuses a vocabulary with no `task_statuses`. Its guard gains 8 mutants. `mockup_records.py` reads the new key; its two vendored copies (qa-flow and design-flow) are byte-identical.
