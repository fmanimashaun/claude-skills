section: design-flow
- **`/design-flow:interface-audit` audits a running app against the interface architecture framework — `plugins/design-flow/commands/interface-audit.md`** (#2221, slice 1 of three; slices 2 and 3 are #2250 and #2251). The new `interface-auditor` agent drives a browser, measures each screen with `interface_collector.js`, and lets scripts decide: `interface_audit.py` judges landmarks (one visible `main`), the skip link and toast region, and `aria-current` on the primary navigation, against rules A1, A2 and A4 of the framework's table. Findings follow a contract, `audit_findings.py`, that refuses a finding naming an unknown rule, carrying a written `authority`, `rank` or `severity`, with no evidence, or about a house rule while claiming a standard; authority is computed from the rule's basis (a verified standard, or a house rule), and the ranking is computed too. `audit_findings.py from-judge` reuses the qa-flow judges' output (`layout_fit.py`, `text_resize.py`, `theme_parity.py`, `link_audit.py`) as findings against A23 to A26 instead of re-measuring. Run in Chrome on a good and a bad fixture: the good page gave no findings and the bad page gave four, two of them labelled house rules. The report lists what it did not audit (verdict rows, #2225; the checks of #2227 to #2230; the mock-up record's lines, #2226). The report prints a **Not audited** section itself, from the rules table (every rule the run did not measure, with what blocks it), carries routes a judge could not verify as unverified rather than clean, and a clean report names the rules it measured and the screens each covered; it never says the interface is good. The contract also refuses a registry whose authority disagrees with its basis (the CLI has no registry option), scans EVERY string of a house-rule finding by a structural walk of the whole object (not a list of fields; a screen must be a path or URL with no spaces and is read with its separators as spaces; the document's title and unverified list are scanned beside house findings) for standard-claiming words, refuses unknown keys at every level, and requires a screenshot to resolve under the run directory.
