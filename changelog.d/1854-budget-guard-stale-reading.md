section: rails-flow
- **The budget guard ignores a usage reading from a window that has already reset, and its refusal says what it knows and how to override — `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/budget-guard.mjs`, `plugins/rails-flow/hooks/context-nudge.mjs`, `plugins/rails-flow/tests/budget-guard.unit.mjs`, `plugins/rails-flow/scripts/mutations/budget_guard.py`** (#1854, owner decision 2026-10-11). On 2026-10-11 at about 15:20 UTC the owner's `/usage` showed the weekly window at 6%, while at least eight long-running sessions refused all new Workflow and Agent calls on a weekly 90–96% that "resets 02:00 UTC": the weekly window had reset at 02:00 UTC and those sessions still held the old window's figure. The declared payload (`SessionRateLimit`: `kind`, `percentUsed`, `resetsAt`; `rateLimits` is "the windows the last response reported", audit `docs/evidence/audits/2026-10-07-mods-tool-call-ratelimits-2.1.292.md`) has no measurement timestamp, so a stale reading cannot be dated from the payload; a past `resetsAt` is the one thing that proves it stale. The hook is advisory, so it now fails open: **a window whose `resetsAt` is at or before now is no reading** (exactly at `resetsAt` counts as expired, a millisecond before does not; a missing or unparsable `resetsAt` cannot be shown stale and keeps the reading), for the weekly refusal and for the usage view and the 5-hour resume timer in the context nudge. The refusal now names the reset time and how long away it is, that the harness gives the reading no timestamp, how long THIS HOOK has seen the same figure (the only age there is; a new figure or window starts again), and the override: "Run /usage; if it disagrees, say so and continue, or set RAILS_FLOW_BUDGET_ALLOW=1 to override." The guard host in the unit test gains a clock and a movable figure; 10 new checks (past, exact and one-millisecond-before `resetsAt`, missing and unparsable `resetsAt`, the message, the age, a new figure restarting it, the usage view and the resume timer) and 10 mutations, all caught; 33 existing mutants checked, 4 retargeted to moved code. Two `session_reset` mutants fail identically on a clean `dev` and are not part of this change. Our own hook; no framework claim.
